Home Dashboard privacy policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Home Dashboard is a private dashboard used by a single household on its own home server. This policy explains what it does with Google account data.

What it accesses

When a household member links their Google account, the dashboard requests access to Google Calendar and to the account's email address. It reads the list of that person's calendars and the events on the calendars they choose to show, and it creates, edits, or deletes events only when someone does so on the dashboard. The email address is only used to label which account is linked.

Where data is kept

The sign-in tokens Google issues, the linked email address, and the list of calendars (names, colors, and which ones are shown) are stored on the household's own server. Calendar events are not saved; they are fetched from Google when the dashboard is displayed and held in memory for about a minute. Nothing is stored with any third party.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models. It is only shown on the household's dashboard to the people who live there.

Home Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Choose Unlink in the dashboard's calendar settings to delete the stored tokens and calendar list for an account. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can go to the site owner through GitHub.
