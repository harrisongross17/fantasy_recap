Home Dashboard

Home Dashboard is a private, self-hosted dashboard for one household. It runs on a home server and is shown on a tablet at home: habit tracking, a shared to-do list, music controls, and the household calendar.

It is not a public service and has no sign-ups. Its Google Calendar connection lets the people in the household see and add events to their own Google calendars from the tablet.
