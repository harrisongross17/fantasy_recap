## Bench Blunder of the Week: Harrison Gross

Harrison Gross picked up **Chris Godwin Jr.** on Friday, Oct. 30, and put him straight into the flex. Godwin scored **0.0**. Sitting on the bench the whole time was **Tyler Allgeier**, who scored **36.1**.

That one call was the game. Harrison lost to Jimmy Miller **118.10 to 106.16**, and Allgeier alone would have put him at 142.26. His best lineup was **143.26**. He left **37.1 points** on the bench, nearly double the league's typical week (19.8).

Allgeier has been a tough guy to keep. Noah Laing dropped him in week 1. Michael Luisi claimed him on Friday, Oct. 16, and dropped him two days later for Malik Washington. Harrison grabbed him on Saturday, Oct. 24, started him in week 7 for 7.9, then benched him for the best game of his season. Allgeier has scored **49.8 points** since Luisi let him go.

Harrison is now 3-5 with two straight losses, and he's left **168.7 points** on his bench this season.

## The Whole League Wants Jonah Coleman

Jonah Coleman was the week's biggest riser, up **36 points** in ESPN ownership to about 77% of leagues. Hayden McMurrey claimed him on waivers on Wednesday, Oct. 28, left him on the bench, and watched him score 20.4. Hayden won anyway, so he'll take it.

Coleman's first fantasy owner was Michael Luisi, who claimed him on Wednesday, Sept. 23, and dropped him two days later for Jared Goff. Coleman then spent four weeks on IR, so that one was defensible. Less defensible is the pattern: this week's top three ownership risers (Coleman, Adonai Mitchell and Tyler Allgeier) were all dropped by Luisi at some point this season.

## Ben Fuller, Above Average and Still Losing

Ben Fuller scored **118.98**, just above the league median of 118.54 and better than five of the ten teams. He drew Hayden McMurrey, who put up 125.46, and lost by 6.48.

The cruel part: Jimmy Miller scored 118.10, less than Ben, and won. Ben is 2-6, but his all-play record of 27-45 says he should have about three wins by now. Ben still leads the all-time series with Hayden 9-6.

## Next Week: Luisi vs. Cooper, Dead Even

For all the roster churn, Michael Luisi just won his third game in four weeks, beating Kevin Mangus **132.80 to 81.48**. Next he gets Jonathan Cooper, and the all-time series is tied **10-10**. Luisi won the last meeting in week 9 last season, 120.84 to 65.50.

Cooper is 6-2 with four straight wins, and for once he didn't win ugly: his 146.48 was the top score of the week. Three of his six wins still came with below-median scores.

Also next week: Kevin Mangus and Jimmy Miller, both 3-5. Jimmy leads the series 6-5 and won the week 1 meeting, 133.52 to 108.30. Jimmy has won two straight; Kevin's 81.48 was the lowest score of week 8.
