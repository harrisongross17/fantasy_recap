## Bench Blunder of the Week: Hayden McMurrey

Hayden McMurrey started **Josh Allen**, who scored **12.86**. On his bench sat **C.J. Stroud**, who scored **35.36**. That's 22.5 points at one position.

It didn't stop there. Hayden started **D'Andre Swift** (3.1) over **Jonah Coleman** (18.8). Last week Hayden benched Coleman for 20.4 and won anyway. This week he benched him again and didn't. Coleman has scored 39.2 points in two weeks on Hayden's roster, all of them from the bench.

Hayden lost to Andrew Hobby **124.68 to 106.76**. His best lineup was **146.96**, which would have won by more than 22. He left **40.2 points** on the bench, well over the league's typical week (15.0).

The strange part is that Hayden is usually the league's best at this. Even after this week, his **132.3** season bench points are the fewest in the league, and his 89.5% lineup efficiency is the best. He's now 6-3 and still leads the all-time series with Hobby 15-13.

## Game of the Week: Ben Fuller Upsets Luke Sullivan

Ben Fuller and Luke Sullivan are rivals, and this week Ben got the better of it. ESPN projected Luke to win **127.97 to 103.82**. Instead Ben won **125.54 to 85.46**, a 40.08-point margin.

Ben beat his projection by 21.72, led by **CeeDee Lamb**'s **33.5**. He did it with **George Pickens** scoring 0.0 in his lineup. Luke went the other way: 42.51 under projection, the worst of the week, and his 85.46 was the week's lowest score. **Josh Jacobs**, who missed Luke's first four games, scored **0.5** in the RB slot while Courtland Sutton (15.5) sat on the bench. Jacobs is now listed as out.

Ben now leads the all-time rivalry **14-10**. He's 3-6, Luke is 4-5 with two straight losses.

## Corey Kiner, Ben's Drop and Luisi's Pickup

The week's biggest ownership riser was **Corey Kiner**, up **31 points** to about 37% of ESPN leagues. Ben Fuller picked him up on Sunday, Nov. 1, started him once for 3.8, and dropped him on Wednesday, Nov. 4 without adding anyone. Kiner then scored 11.5 in week 9 as a free agent.

Michael Luisi claimed him on Wednesday, Nov. 11, and dropped Jared Goff to do it. For once, Luisi is the one picking up somebody else's drop.

Old habits are still around, though. Luisi added **DeMario Douglas** on Wednesday, Nov. 4, dropped him two days later for Woody Marks, watched Douglas score 15.2 as a free agent, and claimed him back on Wednesday, Nov. 11. Douglas was the week's third-biggest riser, up 17 points.

Also rising: **Tank Dell**, up 28 points to about 52%. Hayden McMurrey dropped him back in week 1, and nobody in the league has picked him up since.

## Jonathan Cooper Stops Winning Ugly

Last week's preview called Michael Luisi vs. Jonathan Cooper dead even at 10-10. It wasn't close. Cooper won **174.06 to 102.90**, the week's top score and the third-highest of the season. **Kenneth Walker III** scored 36.3 and **Matthew Golden** 32.4.

The 71.16-point margin is Cooper's biggest win ever over Luisi, and it's the second-biggest blowout of the season, 0.28 short of Luisi's own 71.44 over Jimmy Miller in week 5. Cooper now leads the series **11-10**.

That's **five straight wins**, matching Noah Laing's five to start the season for the longest streak of the year. Noah and Hayden McMurrey both lost, so Cooper is alone in first at **7-2** and moves up from third to first in the power rankings. The team that kept setting the season's lowest winning score has now posted 146.48 and 174.06 in back-to-back weeks.

## Next Week: Jimmy Miller vs. Luke Sullivan

Jimmy Miller lost five straight earlier this season. Now he's won three straight, the latest a **110.70 to 90.12** win over Kevin Mangus. That score was below the week's median of 117.69, so he'll take it.

Next he gets Luke Sullivan, who's lost two straight. Both are 4-5. Jimmy leads the all-time series **7-6**, but Luke won the week 2 meeting, **118.92 to 112.86**.
