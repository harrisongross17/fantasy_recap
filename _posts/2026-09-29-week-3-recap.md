## Standings Watch

Three teams are left unbeaten: **noah laing**, **luke sullivan** and **Hayden McMurrey**. noah laing took the top spot from Andrew Hobby head-to-head, and power rankings agree, moving him to #1. At the other end, Kevin Mangus, Ben Fuller and Michael Luisi are all 0-3.

## Unbeaten Showdown: noah laing 160.68, Andrew Hobby 121.04

The marquee game wasn't close. noah laing put up the week's top score for the **second week in a row** (160.58 in week 2, 160.68 here), with Jahmyr Gibbs scoring 41.4 against a 25.35 projection. Hobby's Jonathan Taylor managed 9.2 on a 20.6 projection, and that was the difference.

The all-time series between these two is now 9-8 in Hobby's favor.

## Luck Check

If every team played every other team every week, **noah laing would be 25-2**. He's legitimately the best team in the league so far.

**Hayden McMurrey** is 3-0, but his all-play record is 17-10. His opponents have scored 72 points *fewer* than the league average this season, the softest schedule in the league. His 102.76 this week was below the league median and still won, by 5.6 over Michael Luisi, the closest game of the season.

**Kevin Mangus** has had the opposite luck. His 124.6 this week was the highest losing score of the season, and his opponents have scored 86 points *more* than the league average this season. He's 0-3, but on scores alone he should have about one win.

## 😬 Bench Blunder of the Week: Ben Fuller (again)

Second blunder award in three weeks. Fuller left **36.3 points on the bench** and posted the week's low score (89.08):
- Started **Jaylen Waddle** (6.4) over **Michael Wilson** (25.9). That's a 19.5-point miss on its own.
- Started **De'Von Achane** (1.7) over **RJ Harvey** (10.7)
- Started **Jadarian Price** (1.2) over **Travis Etienne Jr.** (9.0)

Even an optimal 125.38 wouldn't have caught Harrison Gross's 143.64, but Fuller has now left **105.5 points on the bench this season**, the most in the league. He's starting only 74% of the points he has available.

**Repeat offender:** Harrison Gross won comfortably, but he started **Tetairoa McMillan (3.7) over Christian Watson (22.6)**. That's the exact same call that cost him 22 points in week 1. Watson was a 9th-round pick and is the WR5 in points among drafted receivers. He just needs to be in the lineup.

**Runner-up:** Jonathan Cooper left 35.1 on the bench in a win, starting Kyle Pitts Sr. (1.5) over Harold Fannin Jr. (24.1).

## Waiver Wire

**Michael Luisi's kicker carousel** is now a full storyline: seven kicker stints and five different kickers in under a month.

1. Cam Little, drafted
2. Evan McPherson
3. Cam Little again
4. Cameron Dicker
5. Evan McPherson again
6. Tyler Bass
7. Will Reichard, claimed Wednesday

McPherson, cut twice, has scored 42 points since the first time. Luisi has made **19 pickups** this season; nobody else has more than 5.

The best bit of opportunism came from **Harrison Gross**. Luisi dropped Justin Herbert on Wednesday morning, and Gross picked him up the next morning, along with Bryce Young, after losing Jaxson Dart for the season.

Wednesday's waivers, heading into week 4, are in the Roster Moves section below. Ben Fuller cut De'Von Achane, now on IR, for Alvin Kamara, and noah laing let Marvin Harrison Jr. go for Kalif Raymond.

## Injury Watch

- **luke sullivan** is 3-0 without Josh Jacobs, who hasn't played a snap all season.
- **Michael Luisi's** two highest picks are both stuck. Puka Nacua (round 1) has missed two games and A.J. Brown (round 3) is on IR, and they're the two biggest early-round busts in the draft so far. Nacua is listed questionable for week 4, so help may be on the way. Luisi also stashed rookie Jordyn Tyson, who's on IR and yet to play.
- **Jimmy Miller** is still waiting on Zach Charbonnet, out for all three weeks.
- **Good news:** Brock Bowers returned for Harrison Gross after missing two games and immediately put up 27.6.

## Trade Watch

Three weeks into the preseason Bowers-for-McBride swap: **Trey McBride has 59.1 points for noah laing; Bowers has 27.6 for Harrison Gross**, all of it this week after he missed weeks 1 and 2. If Bowers keeps playing like he did on Sunday, this one tightens up quickly.

## Week 4 Preview

The pick of the week is **Hayden McMurrey vs. noah laing**. That's two 3-0 Whites teams, and a rivalry with three playoff meetings. Hayden leads the all-time series 11-9, but on this season's scoring, noah laing is the favorite.

Also worth watching: **Andrew Hobby vs. luke sullivan**, a chance for Hobby to bounce back against the third unbeaten team. Hobby leads that series 12-6.
