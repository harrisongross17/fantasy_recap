## Standings Watch

Last week three teams were unbeaten. Now there's one: **Noah Laing** is the only 4-0 team, after Luke Sullivan and Hayden McMurrey both took their first losses. At the bottom, **Ben Fuller** and **Kevin Mangus** finally got into the win column, which leaves **Michael Luisi** as the league's only winless team at 0-4. **Jimmy Miller** has now lost three straight.

## Season High: Harrison Gross 178.76, Michael Luisi 109.18

The highest score of the season so far, and the biggest blowout too (69.58). The star was **Tetairoa McMillan: 45.2 points on a 15.85 projection**, the best single-player week of the season. That's a twist, because the running joke this season (weeks 1 and 3) was Harrison starting McMillan over Christian Watson. This time Harrison started both, McMillan carried the team, and Watson scored 7.7.

It could have been even bigger. With Carnell Tate (21.5) and Romeo Doubs (23.8) on the bench, his best possible lineup was **201.56**, which would have been the third-highest score in league history. Harrison now leads the all-time series with Luisi 13-6.

## How Luke Sullivan Lost His Perfect Season

Andrew Hobby won 109.48 to 101.66, and Luke had the answer on his own bench. He started **Blake Corum (3.1)** over **Brian Robinson Jr. (25.7)**, and **Ladd McConkey**, questionable all week, played and scored a flat 0.0. His best lineup scored **135.66**, enough to beat Hobby comfortably and stay unbeaten. Hobby now leads their all-time series 13-7.

## Noah Laing Survives

The last unbeaten team didn't play like one. Noah scored 103.52, **38 points under projection**: Rashee Rice played and scored 0.0 on a 14.9 projection, and Parker Washington added 2.0. That score was below the league median, but Hayden McMurrey managed only 91.92, so it was enough. Hayden now leads that series by just 11-10.

The bigger picture still favors Noah. If everyone played everyone every week, he'd be **28-8**, the best in the league. Hayden is the opposite story: 3-1, but **17-19** against everyone, with opponents scoring 88.5 fewer points than the league average. That's the luckiest record in the league.

## Bench Blunder of the Week: Jimmy Miller

The blunder award moves on from Ben Fuller. Jimmy left **59.2 points on the bench**, the most of any team this week:
- Started **Jake Ferguson** (4.1) over **T.J. Hockenson** (24.9)
- Started **DJ Moore** (2.7) over **Deebo Samuel Sr.** (18.0)
- Started **Emeka Egbuka** (3.3) over **Rome Odunze** (15.4)

Even his best lineup (153.62) would have come up just short of Ben Fuller's 156.52, but that's three losses in a row for Jimmy.

## First Wins

**Ben Fuller** picked a great week to break through: 156.52, beating his projection by 47.4. CeeDee Lamb scored 41.3, and **Emanuel Wilson**, picked up on Friday when Fuller dropped Dallas Goedert, added 27.0 that weekend. Fuller still leads the league in points left on the bench this season (126.0), and Kyle Monangai's 28.0 sat on his bench again this week, but it didn't matter.

**Kevin Mangus** beat Jonathan Cooper 128.08 to 123.20, the **closest game of the season** (4.88). He did it with **Nico Collins' 30.8 points sitting on his bench**, the best bench performance of the week. Cooper's 123.20 was the highest losing score of the week, and Mangus now leads their all-time series 12-4.

## Waiver Wire

- **Michael Luisi's Jared Goff boomerang.** Luisi dropped Goff on 9/30 to claim Braelon Allen. Goff went on to score 20.48 in week 4 while Allen managed 8.7, and on 10/7 Luisi claimed Goff right back.
- **Keenan Allen lasted six hours.** On Friday 10/2, Luisi cut Juwan Johnson, whom he'd claimed just two days earlier, to add Allen. That evening he dropped Allen for Dontayvion Wicks. Johnson scored 11.9 in week 4, and Hayden McMurrey claimed him on 10/7.
- **Alvin Kamara found a home.** Andrew Hobby let him go in week 3, Ben Fuller claimed him the next week after putting De'Von Achane on the cut list, and Kamara put up 22.8 in Fuller's first win.
- **Noah Laing** let Marvin Harrison Jr. go for Kalif Raymond (2.6 points since), and Hobby claimed Harrison Jr. on 10/7. Noah's other claim, Ollie Gordon II, scored 18.0, all of it on Noah's bench.
- Across all of ESPN, **Brian Robinson Jr.**, the back Luke Sullivan benched in his first loss, jumped 17 points in percent rostered this week.

## Injury Watch

- **Noah Laing** heads into week 5 with Rashee Rice, Cam Skattebo and Stefon Diggs all questionable.
- **Luke Sullivan** has still not had Josh Jacobs for a single game (four straight missed), and Caleb Williams has now missed two straight.
- **Jimmy Miller** is still waiting on Zach Charbonnet, out for all four weeks.
- **Harrison Gross** has Brock Bowers questionable again, and Jordan Mason remains on IR.

## Trade Watch

Four weeks into the preseason Bowers-for-McBride swap: **Trey McBride has 69.2 points for Noah Laing, and Brock Bowers has 48.2 for Harrison Gross**. Bowers has scored all of that in the last two weeks after missing the first two, and he's questionable heading into week 5.

## Week 5 Preview

**Harrison Gross (2-2) vs. Noah Laing (4-0).** The season's highest score meets the last unbeaten team. Harrison leads the all-time series 15-10, including one playoff meeting.

Also worth watching:
- **Michael Luisi vs. Jimmy Miller.** One of them ends a losing streak: Luisi's season-long 0-4 or Jimmy's three straight. Jimmy leads the series 6-5.
- **Andrew Hobby vs. Hayden McMurrey,** two 3-1 Whites teams. Hayden leads the series 14-12.
