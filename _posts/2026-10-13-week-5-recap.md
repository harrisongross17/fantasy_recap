## Noah Laing: 193.86, and It Could Have Been More

Last week's preview promised the season's highest scorer against the last unbeaten team. The unbeaten team won, and took the season high too. Noah Laing beat Harrison Gross **193.86 to 136.98**, the highest score of the season and 54.22 over his projection. Jahmyr Gibbs scored 37.7, Jaylen Warren 36.8, and Parker Washington, who managed 2.0 last week, put up 30.4.

The scary part is the bench. Noah started Brock Purdy (16.16) while **Drake Maye scored 39.46 on his bench**, the best QB score of the week. Maye was also the top QB in week 4, and he sat on Noah's bench that week too. Noah's best possible lineup was **224.86**, which would have broken the league's all-time record (Andrew Hobby's 211.12 in 2025).

Harrison's 136.98 is the highest losing score of the season. Only three teams outscored it this week, and Harrison is now 2-3 despite a 28-17 record if everyone played everyone. He still leads the all-time series with Noah, 15-11.

Noah is 5-0 and 37-8 in the all-play standings. Hayden McMurrey, who put up 174.0 to beat Andrew Hobby, is one game back at 4-1.

## Luke Sullivan Benches the Wrong Running Back, Again

Last week Luke started Blake Corum (3.1) over Brian Robinson Jr. (25.7) and lost. This week he flipped it: **Robinson started and scored 1.6, and Corum scored 16.2 on the bench.** Add KC Concepcion (1.5) starting over Courtland Sutton (9.8), and Luke left 22.9 points on the bench.

He lost to Jonathan Cooper **115.46 to 113.62**, by 1.84 points, the closest game of the season. His best lineup scored **136.52**. That's two straight weeks Luke's own bench would have won the game for him. Cooper now leads their all-time series 13-8.

## Michael Luisi Is Off the Schneid

The league's last winless team is winless no more. Michael Luisi beat Jimmy Miller **143.94 to 72.50**, a 71.44-point margin, the biggest blowout of the season. Last week, Luisi was on the wrong end of the previous one.

Puka Nacua, one of the draft's biggest busts through three weeks, scored **37.7**, matching Jahmyr Gibbs for the best non-QB score in the league this week. And **Jared Goff**, whom Luisi dropped on 9/30 and claimed right back on 10/7, scored 28.94 in his first game back.

For Jimmy, 72.50 is the lowest score of the season and his fourth straight loss. Last week he benched T.J. Hockenson (24.9) and started Emeka Egbuka (3.3). This week he did the opposite: Hockenson started and scored 1.7, and Egbuka scored 17.2 on the bench. The all-time series between them is now tied 6-6.

One thing still haunts Luisi: in week 1 he dropped **Rashid Shaheed** for De'Zhaun Stribling. Shaheed has 28.3 points since. Stribling hasn't scored a point since and is now on IR.

## Week 6 Preview

**Jonathan Cooper (3-2) vs. Noah Laing (5-0).** Cooper is coming off the closest win of the season, and Noah is coming off the highest score. Noah leads the all-time series 10-8.
