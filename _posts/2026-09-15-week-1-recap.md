## 😬 Bench Blunder of the Week: noah laing
Won the game, but left a league-worst **43.98 points on the bench** doing it:
- Started **Drake Maye** (9.82) over **Brock Purdy** (21.1) — missed 11.28
- Started **Davante Adams** (5.6) over **Stefon Diggs** (15.5) — missed 9.9
- Started **Cam Skattebo** (14.1) over **Chuba Hubbard** (23.7) — missed 9.6
- Started **Rashee Rice** (9.9) over **Parker Washington** (19.3) — missed 9.4

Four separate "how did you not see this" calls in one lineup. Won anyway, purely on the strength of a good matchup.

**Runner-up in bad lineup management:** Andrew Hobby also left 37.9 on the bench in his win — the two biggest mismanaged rosters of the week both happened to win their games.

**The one that actually hurt:** Harrison Gross left 32.9 on the bench *in a loss*. The single costliest call in the league this week: **Tetairoa McMillan started (10.5) over Christian Watson (32.7)** — a 22.2-point miss on its own. Optimized, Harrison Gross still loses to Andrew Hobby's monster week, but it's a much closer game (149.0 vs 167.56 instead of 116.1 vs 167.56).

**Cleanest roster of the week:** luke sullivan, only 5.9 points left on the table — nearly optimal.
