## Ben Fuller Wins the Closest Game of the Season

Last week's preview promised Michael Luisi vs. Ben Fuller, with the all-time series tied 7-7. It delivered. Ben won **116.48 to 115.26**, a margin of **1.22**, the closest game of the season (the old mark was Jonathan Cooper's 1.84-point win over Luke Sullivan in week 5).

It was an upset, too. Ben was projected for 104.27 and Luisi for 128.15. Joe Burrow (28.88) and CeeDee Lamb (24.4) carried Ben past his projection, while Puka Nacua (21.8) and Braelon Allen (19.4) did most of Luisi's work. Ben now leads the series 8-7, and both teams are 2-5.

## Bench Blunder of the Week: Michael Luisi

Luisi didn't need a better roster to win this one. He needed the one he had. He left **33.46 points on the bench**, with the league's median team leaving 9.7:
- Started **Germie Bernard** (5.7) over **Quinshon Judkins** (23.4)
- Started **A.J. Brown** (7.8) over **Omar Cooper Jr.** (22.5)
- Started **Jordan Love** (16.96) over **Jared Goff** (18.02)

His best lineup was **148.72**, enough to beat Ben by more than 32.

The Omar Cooper Jr. part took some effort. Luisi claimed him on Wednesday, dropped him Friday for Emanuel Wilson, picked him back up Sunday (dropping Wilson), and then left him on the bench for 22.5. The Jared Goff boomerang from week 4 has a sequel.

## The Shaheed Drop Keeps Growing

The week 1 drop is still on the books. **Rashid Shaheed** added 2.1 points this week and now has **42.2** since Luisi cut him for De'Zhaun Stribling, who hasn't scored a point since and is now a free agent on IR. Luisi's week 3 swap of Caleb Douglas for Adonai Mitchell is in the same hole: Douglas has 21.0 since, Mitchell 0.0. That's 63.2 points from two dropped receivers, both still sitting in free agency.

## Jonathan Cooper Wins Ugly Again

Jonathan Cooper beat Harrison Gross **96.80 to 89.32**. Cooper's 96.80 outscored only two teams all week and is now the **lowest winning score of the season**, breaking the record he set last week (100.40). Tee Higgins (26.8) did the heavy lifting while Patrick Mahomes managed 6.0.

Harrison was projected for 136.28 and finished **46.96 under**, the worst miss in the league, with Bryce Young scoring 5.42.

Cooper is 5-2 on a three-game winning streak, tied with Noah Laing and Hayden McMurrey for the best record. His all-play record is 29-34, three of his five wins came with a below-median score, and his opponents have scored 58.15 fewer points than the league average this season. He leads Harrison 14-9 all-time.

## Noah Laing Starts Maye, Loses Anyway

For three straight weeks, Drake Maye outscored Brock Purdy from Noah Laing's bench. This week Noah finally started him, and Maye delivered **27.44** (Purdy scored 12.02 on the bench). Trey McBride added 27.1 and Tre Tucker 21.3. Noah scored **138.54**, more than eight of the other nine teams.

He lost anyway. Jimmy Miller posted the week's top score, **143.92**, behind Tony Pollard's **36.5** (projected 13.81), Amon-Ra St. Brown's 24.2 and Javonte Williams' 21.5. Noah's 138.54 is now the **highest losing score of the season**, passing Harrison Gross's 136.98 from week 5.

Last week's preview called this one a mismatch on paper with Jimmy leading the all-time series 4-1. Jimmy snapped his five-game losing streak and now leads 5-1. Noah is still first with the league's best all-play record (48-15), but he has lost two straight.
