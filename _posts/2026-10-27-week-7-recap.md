## Game of the Week: Ben Fuller by 1.22

Last week's preview promised a coin flip between two teams going in opposite directions, and it delivered. Ben Fuller beat Michael Luisi **116.48 to 115.26**, a 1.22-point margin and the **closest game of the season**.

Ben wasn't supposed to win this one. He was projected for 104.27 against Luisi's 128.15, and he beat his projection by more than 12 points. Joe Burrow scored 28.88, the best QB score of the week, and CeeDee Lamb added 24.4. Ben's lineup was also perfect: he left **zero points on his bench**, the only manager in the league who can say that this week.

The win snaps Ben's two-game skid, both teams are now 2-5, and the all-time series that was tied 7-7 last week now belongs to Ben, 8-7.

## Bench Blunder of the Week: Michael Luisi

Luisi didn't lose this game to Ben so much as to his own bench. He left **33.46 points** sitting there:
- Started **Germie Bernard** (5.7) over **Quinshon Judkins** (23.4)
- Started **A.J. Brown** (7.8) over **Omar Cooper Jr.** (22.5)
- Started **Jordan Love** (16.96) over **Jared Goff** (18.02)

His best lineup was **148.72**, which beats Ben's 116.48 by plenty. Either of the first two swaps on its own would have won the game. Bernard was a waiver claim on Wednesday who went straight into the lineup; Judkins was already on the roster.

## The Whole League Wants Omar Cooper Jr.

Omar Cooper Jr. was the week's biggest riser, up nearly 30 points in ESPN ownership to 62% of leagues. Luisi has him, which is impressive, because Luisi spent most of the week trying not to:
- **Wednesday, Oct. 21:** claimed him on waivers
- **Friday, Oct. 23:** dropped him for Emanuel Wilson
- **Sunday, Oct. 25:** dropped Wilson and picked Cooper back up

Then he left him on the bench, where he scored 22.5. The league is chasing the right guy. Luisi caught him twice and still didn't start him.

## Jonathan Cooper Wins Ugly, Again

Jonathan Cooper beat Harrison Gross **96.80 to 89.32**. That's a new **season record for the lowest winning score**, breaking the one Cooper set himself last week (100.40). His 96.80 beat only two teams this week; the league median was 115.87.

Cooper got a big day from Tee Higgins (26.8) and not much else, with Patrick Mahomes at 6.0. He was lucky Harrison was even colder: Harrison was projected for 136.28 and finished **46.96 under projection**, the worst miss in the league, with Bryce Young scoring 5.42.

That's three straight wins for Cooper, and he's 5-2 with an all-play record of 29-34. Three of his five wins came with a below-median score. Cooper leads the all-time series with Harrison 14-9.

## Noah Laing Starts Maye, Loses Anyway

For three straight weeks, Drake Maye outscored Brock Purdy from Noah Laing's bench. This week Noah finally started Maye, and Maye delivered **27.44** while Purdy scored 12.02 on the bench. Noah lost anyway.

Jimmy Miller beat him **143.92 to 138.54**, with the week's top score. Tony Pollard scored 36.5, more than 22 points over his projection, and Jimmy snapped a five-game losing streak. He now leads the all-time series with Noah 5-1.

Noah's 138.54 beat every team in the league except Jimmy's, and it's the **highest losing score of the season**. He's lost two straight, and he's no longer alone at the top: Noah, Jonathan Cooper and Hayden McMurrey are all 5-2.
