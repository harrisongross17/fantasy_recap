## Noah Laing's Perfect Season Ends

Noah still hasn't started Drake Maye, and this time it cost him. Jonathan Cooper beat Noah **100.40 to 95.86**, Noah's first loss of the season, while **Maye scored 23.8 on Noah's bench** and Brock Purdy scored 17.36 in his place. That's three straight weeks Maye has outscored Purdy from the bench.

It wasn't only the QB. Noah also started Cam Skattebo (0.0) over Woody Marks (10.1) and Davante Adams (6.9, now on IR) over Stefon Diggs (16.4). His best lineup scored **121.90**, which would have won comfortably. He finished 30.72 points under his projection.

Cooper's 100.40 is the lowest winning score of the season, but it's his second straight win, and he's 4-2. Noah now leads their all-time series just 10-9.

Noah is still the top seed, but he has company: Hayden McMurrey posted the week's top score (145.30) and is also 5-1.

## Bench Blunder of the Week: Kevin Mangus

Kevin lost to Harrison Gross **122.74 to 101.98** and left **36.2 points on the bench**, the most in the league:
- Started **Mark Andrews** (1.4) over **Colston Loveland** (18.5)
- Started **Nico Collins** (10.9) over **DK Metcalf** (20.3)
- Started **Rhamondre Stevenson** (3.6) over **Jakobi Meyers** (13.3)

His best lineup was **138.18**, enough to beat Harrison by more than 15. In week 4, Kevin won with Collins' 30.8 sitting on his bench. This time he started Collins (10.9), and Metcalf scored 20.3 on the bench. Kevin is 2-4, and Harrison now leads their all-time series 10-8.

## Michael Luisi Wins Again

The former winless team has a winning streak. Michael Luisi beat Luke Sullivan **108.54 to 86.88** for his second straight win. Luke has now lost three straight and is 3-3. Luisi leads that all-time series 15-6.

Luisi had a QB problem of his own: Trevor Lawrence scored 27.34 on his bench, the best QB score of the week, while Jordan Love started and scored 20.24. And the week 1 drop keeps growing: **Rashid Shaheed** scored another 11.8 and now has 40.1 points since Luisi cut him for De'Zhaun Stribling, who hasn't scored since.

## Jimmy Miller: Five Straight

Jimmy Miller lost to Hayden McMurrey **145.30 to 85.56**, his fifth straight loss, and he's 1-5. He can't be blamed for his lineup: **every player on his bench scored zero**, so he left nothing on it. Tyler Shough scored 26.96, but nothing else could keep up with Hayden's Malik Nabers (28.5), James Cook III (24.3) and D'Andre Swift (23.0). Jimmy still leads that all-time series 7-6.

## Week 7 Preview

**Michael Luisi (2-4) vs. Ben Fuller (1-5).** Luisi has won two straight, Ben has lost two straight, and their all-time series is tied 7-7.

Also worth watching: **Noah Laing (5-1) vs. Jimmy Miller (1-5).** On paper it's a mismatch, but Jimmy leads the all-time series 4-1.
