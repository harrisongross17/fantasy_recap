## Standings Watch

Four teams are still undefeated, and both division leaders (Andrew Hobby in Whites, Luke Sullivan in Blacks) hold the top two spots outright — no standings weirdness to untangle this week.

## 😬 Bench Blunder of the Week: Ben Fuller
Left a league-worst **44.4 points on the bench** — in a loss, which makes this one sting:
- Started **Dallas Goedert** (1.4) over **Dalton Kincaid** (22.5) — missed 21.1
- Started **George Pickens** (10.0) over **Jaylen Waddle** (21.8) — missed 11.8
- Started **Jadarian Price** (5.0) over **De'Von Achane** (12.3) — missed 7.3

Optimized, this lineup scores 140.68 — comfortably beating Jonathan Cooper's 126.74 instead of losing to it.

**Runner-up:** Andrew Hobby left 32.66 on the bench in his win — started Lamar Jackson (14.8) over Dak Prescott (29.76), missing 14.96 on its own.

**Cleanest roster of the week:** Michael Luisi, only 8.8 points left on the table — efficient lineup management in a blowout loss that wasn't his fault.

## Waiver Wire
One real add worth noting: **Andrew Hobby** picked up Rashod Bateman off waivers, dropping Travis Hunter — an actual skill-position swap rather than the usual streaming. Everyone else was mostly cycling kickers and defenses (Jimmy Miller, Ben Fuller, Hayden McMurrey, and Michael Luisi, who's now on his third different kicker/D-ST combo in two weeks).

## Injury Watch
A rough week on the medical front for a few rosters:
- **Harrison Gross** lost Jaxson Dart to a season ending injury.  He also has Jordan Mason on injured reserve, and Brock Bowers listed questionable
- **Ben Fuller** is carrying five questionable-or-worse tags across his roster — the deepest injury list in the league right now
- **Kevin Mangus** and **Jonathan Cooper** are also dealing with multiple bangs and bruises

**Season-long injury tracker** (most flagged players across the two weeks so far): Kevin Mangus leads the league in roster attrition, with Ben Fuller and Jimmy Miller close behind. Worth watching whether this becomes a real storyline as the season goes.

## Trade Watch
Update on the preseason Bowers-for-McBride trade: Brock Bowers is now dealing with a questionable tag on Harrison Gross's roster, while Trey McBride has stayed healthy enough on Noah Laing's roster to start every week without a second thought. Still too early for a final verdict, but Noah Laing has to like his side of this one so far.
