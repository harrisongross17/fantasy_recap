## Game of the Week: Ben Fuller Ends Cooper's Streak

Jonathan Cooper came in on a five-game winning streak, and ESPN projected him to beat Ben Fuller **121.13 to 103.67**. Ben won anyway, **134.86 to 132.26**.

Ben beat his projection by 31.19, the third-best mark of the week. **George Pickens** did most of that (more below), and **Joe Burrow** added **33.16**.

Cooper had the points to win; they were on his bench. He started **Jayden Daniels**, who scored 20.76, while **Patrick Mahomes** sat with **35.42**. That's a 14.66-point swing in a game he lost by 2.60. His best lineup was 147.22. **Ja'Marr Chase**'s 34.1 deserved a better ending.

Cooper still leads the all-time series **17-7**. He's 7-3, and Ben is 4-6 with two straight wins.

## George Pickens, From Zero to 43.5

Last week Pickens scored 0.0 in Ben's lineup. This week ESPN projected him for 12.25, and he scored **43.5**, beating that by **31.25**, the biggest overperformance of the week.

## Jonathan Cooper, Unlucky for Once

All season the joke has been that Cooper wins ugly: three of his seven wins came with below-median scores. This week he flipped it. His **132.26** beat the week's median of 125.95 and outscored six of the other nine teams, and he still lost. It's the third-highest losing score of the season.

He's had a soft schedule all year, though. His **1,118.4** points against are the fewest in the league, 81.3 below the league average for the season. With Noah Laing's 181.26 win, Noah and Cooper are both 7-3, and Noah moves back to first in the power rankings.

## Luke Sullivan by 0.72

Last week's preview had Jimmy Miller, winner of three straight, against Luke Sullivan, loser of two straight. Luke won **114.80 to 114.08**. The **0.72**-point margin is the closest game of the season, beating Ben Fuller's 1.22-point win over Michael Luisi in week 7.

Luke wasn't great. His 114.80 was below the week's median and outscored only three teams, and **Kyren Williams** scored 5.2 on a projection of 18.63. But **Jaxon Smith-Njigba** scored **31.1**, and Luke didn't leave a single point on his bench. In a game decided by 0.72, that mattered.

Jimmy's streak ends at three. The all-time series, which Jimmy led 7-6, is now tied **7-7**. Luke is 5-5, and Jimmy is 4-6.

## Bench Blunder of the Week: Jimmy Miller

Jimmy didn't need a perfect lineup. He started **Javonte Williams**, who scored **7.7**, while **Deebo Samuel Sr.** sat on his bench with **25.9**. That's **18.2** points left on the bench, in a game he lost by 0.72.

His best lineup was **132.28**, which would have beaten Luke by more than 17. Jimmy has now left **180.2** points on his bench this season.
